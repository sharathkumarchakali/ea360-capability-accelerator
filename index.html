Frequently asked questions

Is this production software?

No. EA360 here is a Kulana product prototype with synthetic data for demonstrations and discovery workshops.

Does the prototype use real customer data?

No institutional data is loaded. All metrics, entities and narratives are illustrative and labelled accordingly.

Which organisations are included?

Demonstration tenant packs cover Ghana Revenue Authority, Bank of Ghana, Fidelity Bank Ghana and a generic enterprise profile.

How do I start the demo?

Select Open prototype, choose a tenant and role, then follow the guided demo bar or explore modules freely.
